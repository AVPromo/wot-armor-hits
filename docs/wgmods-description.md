# Armor Hits

Your battle hits on the 3D vehicle in the hangar:

- hits on you and by you, filtered by result: damage, blocked, ricochet, no damage;
- the shell's way to the armor, turret and gun turned as at the moment of the hit;
- shooter view: the camera along the shell;
- damage, penetration, armor, angle, distance, damaged modules and crew;
- the game's armor views and armor tooltip;
- battles kept for the session or on disk.

## Installation

![Installation: copy the mods folder from the archive into the game folder](install-guide.png)

1. Open the archive.
2. Copy the `mods` folder into the game folder, where `WorldOfTanks.exe` is. Replace files if asked.
3. Check that the files are in the folders shown in the picture.
4. Start the game and click the **Armor Hits** button in the hangar.

## FAQ

**1. Why is my last battle not in the list?**

Its mode group is set to **Off** in **Keep battles**. By default only Random and Competitive battles are kept.

**2. Where are the saved battles?**

In `[WoT game directory]/mods/sagtary_armor_hits/hits/`, one file per battle. The files stay on your computer; they
hold the names of the players you hit and who hit you.
